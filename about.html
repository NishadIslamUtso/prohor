Prohor — BRACU routine planner

Prohor is a free, unofficial routine planner for BRAC University students. Compare course sections, avoid class and exam clashes, choose faculty and times, and export a weekly schedule.

Plan and compare routines

Select up to six courses and filter their time slots, sections or faculty. Set maximum campus days and unavailable times, then generate combinations without overlapping classes or labs. Exam-clash checking is enabled by default. Rank the routines found so far and request more results when needed.

Check sections and seats

The seats panel shows published availability and lets you pin sections. Seat availability is ignored for routine generation by default; you can instead prefer or require available seats. The semester selector also provides collected faculty and section observations. Saved data may be older than the live feed and may retain sections that have since disappeared.

Export and share

Copy a section list, print results, download a routine as a PNG, or share a link to your course selections and preferences. Your planner settings are saved in your browser. Semester collection is stored separately in the project repository.

Independent student project

Prohor is not affiliated with BRAC University. Its source data comes from Connect-CDN and can be delayed or unavailable. Always confirm registration details in BRACU Connect.
