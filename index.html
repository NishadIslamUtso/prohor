How Prohor works

A prohor (প্রহর) is one-eighth of a day. Pick yours in three steps:

  1. Pick your courses. Add up to six — lock slots, sections or faculty per course if you care.
  2. Set your preferences. Days on campus, exam-clash checks, times and days to avoid.
  3. Compare routines. Every conflict-free combination, ranked. Download any as a PNG.

Open Seats for a panel that splits the screen — drag its edge to size it, tap it to fold to a strip, pin sections to keep their seat counts on top. It also stands alone as a page (?view=seats). Anything unexplained is one hover away. Shortcuts: / search · Ctrl/⌘ + Enter generate.
