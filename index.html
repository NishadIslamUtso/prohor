How Prohor works

A prohor (প্রহর) is one-eighth of a day. Pick yours in three steps:

  1. Pick your courses. Search, add up to six. Lock time slots or faculty per course if you care.
  2. Set your preferences. How many days on campus, exam-clash checks, times and days to avoid.
  3. Compare routines. Every conflict-free combination, ranked. Download any as PNG.

Shortcuts: / focus course search · Ctrl/⌘ + Enter generate
